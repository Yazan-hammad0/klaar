What Klaar is

Klaar is an independent practice app for the Dutch car theory exam and for the Dutch integration exam (inburgering). It has lessons, practice questions and mock exams.

Klaar is not the CBR or DUO and is not linked to them. The questions are written by us in the style of the real exams. They are not official exam questions. For official rules, dates, prices and bookings, always use cbr.nl, inburgeren.nl and duo.nl.

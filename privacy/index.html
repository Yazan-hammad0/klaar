Privacy

You can use Klaar without an account: then your progress stays on your phone. If you choose to make an account, a copy of your progress is saved in the EU so you can use it on another phone. We count how the app is used, without knowing who you are, so we can fix the hard parts.

Who is responsible

[Company name], KVK [KVK number], [business address], is responsible for your data (the "controller" under the GDPR). Email: [contact email].

What stays on your phone

Your answers, your progress, your exam date and the name you may have typed are saved in your browser, on your own device. Without an account they are never sent to us. We cannot see them and we cannot get them back for you if you lose them.

To delete them, use Start again in your profile, or remove the app's data in your browser settings.

If you make an account

An account is optional. You make one in your profile with Save your progress. We then store:

  • Your email address and your password, stored only as a one-way hash we cannot read back
  • A copy of your progress: your answers per question, mock exam scores, practice days, your settings, your exam dates, and the first name if you typed one
  • Sign-in times and the technical data needed to keep you signed in

Why: so your progress is safe if you lose your phone, and so you can use it on another phone or after Klaar moves to a new web address. The legal basis is the agreement you make with us when you create the account (GDPR article 6(1)(b)). We do not use this data for anything else, and we never sell it.

Your email is not checked and we do not send you email, except to answer you if you write to us.

Where it is stored

Accounts and saved progress are stored by Supabase, on servers in the European Union (Ireland). Supabase is our processor: it stores the data for us, under a data processing agreement, and may not use it for itself.

Only you, when signed in, can read or change your saved progress. We can see it in the Supabase dashboard if we need to fix a problem.

How long we keep it

  • As long as you have an account.
  • If you delete your account, your email, password hash and saved progress are removed from the database straight away. Supabase's backups are overwritten within [backup period, for example 7 days].
  • Accounts not used for [2 years] may be deleted. [Confirm before launch.]

Deleting your account

Open your profile in the app and tap Delete account, then tap again to confirm. This removes your account and the progress saved to it from our servers. The progress on your phone stays, so you can keep practising; remove it with Start again or in your browser settings.

Can't sign in any more? Email [contact email] from the address you used and we delete it for you.

What we count

Klaar sends anonymous counts to GoatCounter, a small privacy-friendly statistics service:

  • that the app was opened, and whether from the home screen or the browser
  • which screen you opened
  • which question was answered, and whether it was right or wrong
  • that a lesson was finished or a mock exam was passed or failed, with the score
  • your screen size, browser type, language and rough location (country)

There is no name, no email, no cookie and no code that follows you from one visit to the next, and these counts are never linked to your account. GoatCounter does not store your IP address.

You can switch this off at any time: open your profile in the app and tap Turn off anonymous stats.

When you report a question

If you tap Report a problem with this question, the question number and the reason you picked are sent the same anonymous way. This also happens when stats are switched off, because you asked for it. Nothing about you is added.

Hosting

The app is served by GitHub Pages. [After the move: Cloudflare Pages, with a link to Cloudflare's privacy policy.] Like every web host, GitHub sees your IP address when your phone downloads the pages and may keep it in its logs. See the GitHub privacy statement.

What we do not do

No ads. No advertising trackers. No selling or sharing of data. No email list you get added to without asking. No payment data: there is no paid plan yet.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • see what we store about you, and get a copy (in the app: profile, Save backup, gives you all of your progress as a file)
  • have it corrected
  • have it deleted (profile, Delete account)
  • take it with you to another service
  • object to or limit how we use it

Email [contact email] for any of these. We answer within one month. If you are not happy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Dutch data protection authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

Without an account, Klaar holds nothing that can be traced to you. Everything personal is then on your phone, where you can delete it yourself.

About the questions

Klaar is an independent practice app. It is not affiliated with the CBR, and the questions are not official exam questions. Traffic rules change. Each answer links to its source where there is one, so you can check it. Practising with Klaar does not guarantee that you pass.

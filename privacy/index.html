Privacy

Klaar has no accounts. Your progress stays on your phone. We count how the app is used, without knowing who you are, so we can fix the hard parts.

What stays on your phone

Your answers, your progress, your exam date and the name you may have typed are saved in your browser, on your own device. They are never sent to us. We cannot see them and we cannot get them back for you if you lose them.

To delete them, use Start again in your profile, or remove the app's data in your browser settings.

What we count

Klaar sends anonymous counts to GoatCounter, a small privacy-friendly statistics service:

  • that the app was opened, and whether from the home screen or the browser
  • which screen you opened
  • which question was answered, and whether it was right or wrong
  • that a lesson was finished or a mock exam was passed or failed, with the score
  • your screen size, browser type, language and rough location (country)

There is no name, no account, no cookie and no code that follows you from one visit to the next. GoatCounter does not store your IP address.

You can switch this off at any time: open your profile in the app and tap Turn off anonymous stats.

When you report a question

If you tap Report a problem with this question, the question number and the reason you picked are sent the same anonymous way. This also happens when stats are switched off, because you asked for it. Nothing about you is added.

Hosting

The app is served by GitHub Pages. Like every web host, GitHub sees your IP address when your phone downloads the pages and may keep it in its logs. See the GitHub privacy statement.

What we do not do

No ads. No advertising trackers. No selling or sharing of data. No email list you get added to without asking.

Your rights

Under European privacy law you may ask what is stored about you and have it corrected or deleted. Klaar holds nothing that can be traced to you, so there is nothing on our side to look up. Everything personal is on your phone, where you can delete it yourself.

About the questions

Klaar is an independent practice app. It is not affiliated with the CBR, and the questions are not official exam questions. Traffic rules change. Each answer links to its source where there is one, so you can check it. Practising with Klaar does not guarantee that you pass.

Questions or a mistake to report

Open an issue on the project page on GitHub.
